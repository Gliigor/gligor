# Fonts

The `.woff` files in this folder are the Latin subsets of open-source fonts,
copied from the [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org) npm packages. They are
embedded in generated PDF reports.

All fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), which permits bundling and embedding:
